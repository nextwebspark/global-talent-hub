### Notes on Sources & Data Gaps
* **Revenue data** is publicly available for only a subset of the distributors (Al Seer Group, Al Maya, Binzagr, Al Seer ASG). Many large private distributors (e.g., Gulfco, KACS) do not disclose standalone turnover, which is common in the GCC market.  
* **Employee counts** come from company websites, LinkedIn, or business-information platforms (Datanyze, RocketReach, Clodura).  
* **Executive listings** are taken from the companies’ “About us” pages, LinkedIn profiles, or corporate PDFs. When a full C-suite list was not available, the most senior roles identified are shown.

### How the List Was Compiled
1. Searched for “top FMCG distributors UAE” and “top FMCG distributors Saudi Arabia” and extracted companies described explicitly as **distributors** rather than retailers or manufacturers.  
2. Cross-checked each candidate against business-information databases (Datanyze, RocketReach, Clodura) for employee headcounts and executive names.  
3. Retrieved the latest revenue figures from annual reports, company PDFs, or reputable market-intel sites (e.g., Owler, RocketReach).

### Recommendations for Further Validation
* For companies lacking disclosed revenue (e.g., Gulfco, KACS), consider requesting financial summaries directly from the firms or consulting paid market-research databases (e.g., Bloomberg, S&P Capital IQ).  
* Verify executive turnover by checking the most recent press releases or LinkedIn updates, as leadership can change frequently in the fast-moving consumer-goods sector.
