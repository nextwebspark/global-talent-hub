6. Strategic Market Observations

The CEO market in luxury retail is relatively concentrated among family-linked and heritage brands.

UAE presents strong regional leadership depth with higher mobility.

Swiss leaders demonstrate premium compensation but lower flexibility.

Asian executives (HK / Singapore) show higher openness to relocation.

Compensation clustering suggests realistic CEO band: $350K–$420K total package.
