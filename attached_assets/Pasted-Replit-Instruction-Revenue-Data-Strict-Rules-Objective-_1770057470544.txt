Replit Instruction — Revenue Data (Strict Rules)
Objective

Ensure Revenue is always accurate, comparable, and never confused with other financial figures.

1. Definition (Non-Negotiable)

Revenue means:

Top-line operating revenue

Generated from normal business activities

Reported for a specific financial year

Revenue does not include:

Project value or contract value

Capital injections or funding amounts

Assets under management (AUM)

Assets under development (AUD)

Gross merchandise value (GMV)

Valuation or enterprise value

Pipeline or backlog value

Investment size or capex

If a number is not explicitly stated as revenue, it must not be treated as revenue.

2. Source Priority (Mandatory)

Revenue must be sourced in the following order only:

Audited annual reports or regulatory filings

Official company financial disclosures

Clearly labelled revenue figures from trusted aggregators

If none of the above explicitly state revenue:

Revenue must be set to “Unknown”

Do not infer or convert from other figures

3. Time Period Rule

Revenue must always relate to the latest available financial year

The year must be clearly stored and displayed

Historical revenue must not be shown as current

If the year is unclear, revenue must not be displayed.

4. No Substitutions, No Proxies

The system must never substitute:

Project value → revenue

AUM → revenue

GMV → revenue

Funding → revenue

Even if no revenue is available.

Showing no revenue is acceptable. Showing the wrong revenue is not.

5. Labelling & Transparency

Every revenue figure must store and display:

Revenue value

Currency

Financial year

Source

Confidence level

If any of these are missing, the revenue figure must not be displayed.

6. Conflict Handling

If multiple revenue figures exist:

Prefer the most authoritative source

Prefer the most recent financial year

If conflict remains → flag for review, do not guess

7. Explicit Prohibition

❌ Do not infer revenue
❌ Do not calculate revenue
❌ Do not restate revenue from related metrics
❌ Do not label any other financial figure as revenue

Final Rule (Very Important)

No revenue is better than wrong revenue.

This rule overrides all UI, completeness, or “niceness” considerations.
