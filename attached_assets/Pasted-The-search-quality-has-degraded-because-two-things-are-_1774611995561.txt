The search quality has degraded because two things are broken simultaneously. Both need fixing together:
Fix 1 — Restore minimal context for classification without full enrichment
When the enrichment was removed, classification quality dropped significantly because Claude now has insufficient information to make accurate tier decisions. Restore a lightweight classification call that fetches only: what is this company's primary business activity, and does it pass the three-tier test. This is a single focused Claude call per company — not the full enrichment with revenue, headcount, website etc. The full enrichment stays removed and happens later inside the project. But the classification needs enough context to work correctly.
Fix 2 — Adjacent sector queries must anchor to the commercial role, not just the sector
Every adjacent sector query is currently finding sector leaders generally rather than distribution-adjacent companies specifically. The query construction must always append the commercial role context. Instead of searching "Personal Care & Cosmetics companies in UAE", search "Personal Care & Cosmetics distribution companies in UAE" — and apply the same transferability test: would a senior executive here be credible in an FMCG distribution leadership role?
